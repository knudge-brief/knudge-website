This Privacy Policy explains how Knudge ("Knudge", "we", "us", or
"our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information about you when
you use the Knudge mobile application and related services
(collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the
collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

The Service is currently available in India and select regions only. If
you are accessing the Service from a jurisdiction where it is not
officially available, you do so at your own risk and we make no
representations that the Service complies with the laws of your
jurisdiction.

## Our Core Privacy Commitments

Before the detail, here is what we commit to in plain language:

-   We collect only what is necessary to personalize your news
    experience.

-   All personal data you provide is encrypted at rest and in transit.
    It is stored securely on our servers and is not shared with any
    third party, including advertisers.

-   We will never sell your data. Ever.

-   When we show you sponsored content, the matching happens entirely on
    our servers using your profile. Advertisers never see your data -
    not even in anonymized form tied to you individually.

-   You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Data Minimisation Principle

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the Service. Every
data point collected through onboarding serves a specific, documented
purpose: personalizing your news feed. We do not collect data
speculatively or for purposes beyond those stated in this Policy.

### 1.2 Information You Provide During Onboarding

To personalize your news feed, Knudge collects the following through our
onboarding questionnaire:

-   Work and employment status (e.g., salaried, founder, freelancer,
    student)

-   Industry sector(s) you work in

-   Financial profile information, including loan types, investment
    holdings, insurance coverage, and tax regime preference

-   Housing and vehicle ownership status

-   Family situation and dependent structure

-   Travel patterns and international ties

-   Lifestyle and personal interests

-   Current life situations and short-term circumstances ("transient
    flags"), such as job seeking, expecting a child, or planning a home
    purchase

-   Topic and lifestyle preferences selected via the topic and lifestyle
    bubble screens

Some of this information is sensitive in nature - particularly
health-related flags (e.g., managing a chronic condition) and financial
flags. We collect this data solely for the purpose of personalizing your
news experience. We do not use it for credit scoring, insurance
underwriting, employment screening, or any purpose other than content
personalization and directly related analytics.

### 1.3 Authentication Information

If you sign in using Google or Apple, we receive your name and email
address from those providers. We do not receive your password. We use
this information solely to create and manage your account.

### 1.4 Usage and Reading Behaviour

We automatically collect information about how you interact with the
Service, including:

-   Stories you open, skip, or spend time reading

-   Topics and categories you engage with most

-   App open frequency, session duration, and navigation patterns

-   Device type, operating system, and app version

-   City-level location, inferred from IP address. We do not access your
    GPS or precise location.

### 1.5 Push Notification Tokens

If you grant notification permissions, we collect your device push token
to deliver your daily brief and relevant alerts. You can revoke this
permission at any time through your device settings.

### 1.6 App Permissions We Request

The Knudge app requests the following device permissions:

-   Notifications - to deliver your daily brief and relevant alerts.
    Required for core functionality.

-   Internet access - to fetch news content and sync your profile.
    Required for core functionality.

-   Local storage / cache - to store your feed locally for faster load
    times. No personal data is stored unencrypted on your device.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise
GPS location, or any other device capability beyond the above.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

-   To personalize your news feed based on your declared profile flags
    and reading behaviour

-   To deliver your daily brief via push notification

-   To improve our editorial curation, story scoring, and content
    recommendation algorithms

-   To show you Native Sponsored Insights - contextually relevant
    content funded by brand partners, matched to your profile flags
    entirely on our servers (see Section 4)

-   To analyse aggregate, anonymized usage patterns and improve the
    Service

-   To communicate with you about product updates, changes to this
    Policy, or account matters

-   To comply with applicable law

### 2.1 AI-Assisted Curation - How We Use Your Data with AI

Knudge uses AI and machine learning systems to curate, summarise, and
rank news stories for your feed. Regarding AI and your data:

-   Your personal profile flags and reading behaviour are used to rank
    and personalize content within our own systems only.

-   We do not share your personal data with any third-party AI provider
    for model training purposes.

-   We do not use your personal data to train any external AI model.

-   Aggregated, anonymized, non-identifiable patterns may be used to
    improve our own internal recommendation algorithms.

AI-generated or AI-assisted summaries may occasionally contain errors or
omissions. Do not rely on Knudge content for financial, medical, legal,
or other consequential decisions without independent verification.

## 3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your profile data and reading history for as long as your
account is active.

### 3.1 Account Deletion and the 30-Day Grace Period

If you choose to delete your account via Settings > Delete Account:

-   Your account enters a 30-day deactivation window. During this
    period, your account and data are suspended but not permanently
    deleted.

-   If you log back in within 30 days, your account is fully reactivated
    and all your data is restored exactly as it was.

-   If you do not log in within 30 days, your account and all associated
    personal data - including profile flags, reading history, and
    notification tokens - are permanently and irreversibly deleted from
    our systems.

-   This permanent deletion cannot be undone. You will be clearly
    informed of this at the point of initiating deletion in the app.

Transient flags (time-bounded life situations) are automatically decayed
or reset on the schedule disclosed in the app, typically within 30 to
180 days depending on the flag type, regardless of account deletion.

## 4. Native Sponsored Insights - Our Walled Garden Model

Knudge shows Native Sponsored Insights - short editorial-style cards
funded by brand partners. These are clearly labelled as "Sponsored" or
"In partnership with [Brand]" within the app.

Our advertising model is built on a strict walled garden principle. This
means:

-   All ad targeting happens entirely on Knudge's servers. We match
    sponsored cards to your profile flags internally - no data leaves
    our systems for this purpose.

-   Your personal data - including your profile flags, reading history,
    and any other information you have shared with us - is never shared
    with, exported to, or accessible by any advertiser or brand partner.
    Not in identifiable form. Not in anonymized form tied to you
    individually.

-   Advertisers receive only campaign-level aggregate metrics: for
    example, how many users in a given flag cohort were shown a card,
    and how many tapped through. These metrics cannot be used to
    identify any individual user.

-   We do not sell your data. We do not broker your data. We do not
    allow advertisers to upload audience lists for matching against our
    users.

-   Your data stays with Knudge. Full stop.

Native Sponsored Insights are a core part of the Knudge experience. By
using the Service, you acknowledge that you will see sponsored content
matched to your profile. This matching happens entirely on our servers -
no data is shared with advertisers.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Beyond the walled garden advertising
model described in Section 4, we share information only in the following
limited circumstances:

-   Infrastructure and service providers: We use third-party vendors for
    cloud hosting, push notification delivery, and app analytics. These
    vendors process data only on our instructions, under data processing
    agreements, and are not permitted to use your data for any other
    purpose. All data shared with infrastructure providers is encrypted.

-   Third-party login providers: If you use Google or Apple Sign-In,
    those providers have their own privacy policies governing their
    handling of your authentication information.

-   Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by
    applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority, or to protect
    the rights and safety of Knudge, its users, or others.

-   Business transfers: If Knudge is acquired or merges with another
    entity, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We
    will notify you at least 30 days before your data becomes subject to
    a different privacy policy and give you the option to delete your
    account.

## 6. Data Security and Encryption

We treat the security of your data as a core product requirement, not an
afterthought. Our security measures include:

-   Encryption at rest: All personal data stored on our servers is
    encrypted using industry-standard encryption.

-   Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your device and
    our servers is protected using TLS (Transport Layer Security).

-   Access controls: Access to user data within our systems is
    restricted on a need-to-know basis and logged.

-   No plaintext export: Your profile flags and personal data are never
    exported in plaintext to any external system, including advertiser
    dashboards.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we
take every reasonable measure to protect your data, we cannot guarantee
absolute security. In the event of a data breach, all stored data is
encrypted, meaning raw personal data is not directly exposed even in a
worst-case scenario. However, we will notify you and the relevant
authorities of any breach that is likely to affect your rights or
freedoms, as required by applicable law.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding
your personal data:

-   Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

-   Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

-   Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal
    retention obligations and the 30-day grace period described in
    Section 3).

-   Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable
    format.

-   Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

-   Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for sensitive data
    processing at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior
    processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer at the
address listed in Section 10. We will acknowledge your request within 24
hours and respond within 30 days.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Knudge is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 where
required by applicable law). We do not knowingly collect personal data
from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you
of material changes via push notification or a prominent in-app notice
at least 7 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of the
Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated
Policy. If you do not agree with the changes, you may delete your
account before the effective date.
